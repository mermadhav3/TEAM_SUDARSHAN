Overview

SUDARSHAN is a concept-stage precision agriculture system designed to integrate multi-point soil sensing, offline data processing, automated fertigation, motor safety, and a multilingual farmer application.

The project aims to explore how soil data and automated control can support more informed irrigation and fertilizer management decisions.

Problem Statement

Farmers may face challenges related to soil variability, water management, fertilizer usage, and irrigation motor safety. Affordable, accessible solutions that combine soil information with irrigation and nutrient management could help address these challenges.

Proposed System Modules

1. Multi-Point Soil Sensing: Proposed collection of NPK, pH, and moisture readings from multiple field locations.
2. Portable Device & Local Gateway: Local data transfer using Bluetooth/Wi-Fi and proposed offline processing.
3. Automated Fertigation & Nutrient Control: Proposed controller-based nutrient dosing and solenoid-valve operation.
4. Motor Safety & Water Control: Water-level monitoring and proposed low-water motor protection.
5. Multilingual Farmer App: Intended display of soil values, alerts, motor status, and language options.

UN Sustainable Development Goals

The project explores potential alignment with SDGs 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 12, 13, 15, and 17 through sustainable agriculture, efficient resource use, innovation, farmer education, inclusion, and future rural livelihood opportunities.

Development Roadmap

1. Finalise the technical architecture and control logic.
2. Select suitable sensors, controller, valves, and dosing components.
3. Develop and integrate the physical prototype.
4. Test sensor readings, communication, dosing, and motor safety.
5. Validate performance under field conditions.

Current Project Status

Concept and proposed design stage. The physical prototype has not yet been built or tested. Performance, resource savings, and real-world impact remain to be validated.

Future Scope

Prototype development, experimental testing, field validation, and evaluation of resource efficiency and system reliability.
